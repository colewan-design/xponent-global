Bits, Casing Shoes and Reamers

Xponent Global’s Diamond bits are highly versatile – capable of adjusting from one ground formation to another. They are also very durable, able to resist the wear and punishment of extreme ground conditions.

We use the latest technological advances to keep up with the industry demand to produce reliable, high production bits at a competitive price for even the harshest of drilling conditions.

Our Bits are commonly used to perform geotechnical coring, and material sample coring and mineral exploration.
